Tadashi Samo owned a tea stall, which never got more than enough customers to feed themselves, yet they would rather go hungry than not feed a guest. Tadashi Samo woke at 7 am on a Tuesday. An hour later, he was up and ready at his tea stall. It was just like how Tadashi inherited it from his father and his grandfather and his great great grandfather. A dirt road in the front, Never-ending green meadows in the open, and just his and his wife’s house in the distance. He was nostalgic for his childhood and worried that no customers might buy anything, until he noticed something peculiar. Four people were walking down the dirt path. Worst of all, they were different colors, Red, Blue, Green, and Yellow. “Maybe, this isn’t my average morning.” thought Tadashi. “How much for four cups?” asked the Red one. “20” they both said simultaneously. Now, Tadashi was scared out of his wits. “First, they were colored. Now, they’re reading my mind.” Tadashi thought. “You’ve got to stop scaring people, Red.” said Blue. “Ah, it’s not even fun anymore” Red replied. “Do you want four cups of tea, sir?” asked Tadashi, who was getting more and more impatient. “Yeah, whatever” said Yellow and tossed some coins “Keep the change”. “What’s the time and place again?” asked Green. “Its 7th of May, 1010, and we’re in Hokkaido, Japan, sir.” Tadashi replied. “Have you seen Purple anywhere yet, Green?” as There’s ked Red. “No, not at all” Green replied. “Purple? more?” Tadashi wondered. Tadashi was now getting more and more interested by these colored people. All his life, he had only seen grey, or what he thought, non-colored people. Seeing different colored people was piquing his curiosity. Tadashi saw a purplish object zooming across the sky, or so he thought. As Tadashi was about to speak, three golden nets dropped out of nowhere and trapped blue, green and yellow. “Wha-?” said Red, and a purple-colored person floated down. “YOU!” shouted Red, and tried to punch him, but Purple dashed away. “You’re good, BUT NOT GOOD ENOUGH! Without them, you’re just useless.” Purple said, but Red seemed unfazed. Then, Purple started punching Red but Red failed to block it. Then Purple grabbed Red by the neck, and zoomed into the horizon, only to come back down and throw Red against the ground. Red was severely bleeding and bruised up. You could tell he was almost dead. “Not my normal morning” Tadashi told himself. “Giving up so easy? You aren’t like you used to be.” Said purple and was about to finish him off with a blow, but Red rolled to the side and got up, and said “Sacrificial Exchange: Red, Blue, Green and Yellow for the Holy seal of one thousand and sixteen years used upon Purple and Reincarnation of Red, Blue, Green and Yellow in one thousand years.”. “WAIT! NO!” yelled Purple and desperately tried to attack Red, but it was too late. An orange band of fire with ancient Japanese symbols on it wrapped around Purple, instantly freezing him on the spot and making a bright orange sphere. Then it shrank to the size of a tennis ball and turned Purple. Red, Blue, Green and Yellow dissipated into thin air right in front of Tadashi. Tadashi pinched himself to check if it was a dream or not, but unfortunately, it wasn’t. After a one thousand and fifteen years, in Tokyo, a boy named Red woke up with a start.
